Booking Policies

Please read before booking.

PAYMENT

I currently accept Cash, Cash App, Apple Pay and Venmo. Other forms of payment will not be accepted.

GUESTS

Due to limited space, please do not bring additional guests unless previously discussed.

LATE POLICY

A 15 min grace period is provided. A $10 late fee applies after the grace period. Arriving 30 min late results in cancellation.

CANCELLATION

A minimum of 24 hours notice is required for cancellation or rescheduling.

Before your appointment
A non-refundable $10 deposit is required to secure your appointment. No deposit = no appointment.
Please come washed & blow dried and free of product for best results.
Keep your schedule open around your appointment. I take my time on each style to focus on neatness, detail and quality, so times may vary.
Bring any specific hair or accessories needed for your style if they aren’t provided by me.
Have your desired hairstyle and any reference pictures ready before your appointment.
Questions, concerns or changes? Please reach out beforehand.
