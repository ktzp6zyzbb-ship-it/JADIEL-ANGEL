Sweet Questions

How do I place an order?

Send a DM to @damiyahscookiecrush on Instagram with your flavors, quantity, and pickup date. You can use the order form above to write the message for you.

Are the cookies really homemade?

Yes! Every cookie is baked by hand in a home kitchen in Richmond, VA.

What payment methods do you take?

Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, and cash.

Where do I pick up my order?

Pickup is in the VCU area of Richmond. You'll get the exact spot and time in your DMs after your order is confirmed.

How far ahead should I order?

The earlier the better — since everything is baked to order, booking a few days ahead helps make sure your date is open. Big party orders should book further out.

Can I get custom flavors or colors?

Ask in your DM! Custom colors for birthdays, graduations, and events are always fun to make.
