What we tow

Towing & transport

One flatbed rollback handles most of what goes wrong with a car, plus the moves you plan ahead.

  • Breakdowns

    Won't start, overheated, or died on the shoulder. Darwin picks it up and takes it to your mechanic or home.

  • Accident towing

    After a collision he loads the car and takes it to the body shop, the insurance lot, or wherever you choose.

  • Vehicle transport

    Moving a car you just bought, sold, or need at the dealer, auction, or a shop across town.

  • Low & AWD cars

    All-wheel drive, lowered, and luxury cars ride flat with all four wheels off the road. Nothing drags.

  • Non-running cars

    A car that has been sitting, won't roll, or is headed to the junkyard gets winched onto the bed.

  • Local & long distance

    Across town or out of the area. Call with both addresses and Darwin will give you a quote.

How it works

From the call to drop-off

  1. Call or text 571-568-1530

    Darwin answers his own phone. If he's mid-tow, text and he'll get right back to you.

  2. Say where you are

    An address, a cross street, or the highway, direction, and nearest exit. Then where the car is going and what you drive.

  3. Darwin drives to you

    He tells you about when he'll arrive. Wait somewhere safe and keep your phone on.

  4. Loaded, strapped, delivered

    The car is winched onto the bed, strapped at the wheels, and dropped where you asked.

While you wait

Stay safe on the roadside

  • Turn on your hazard lights.
  • On a highway, get out on the side away from traffic and stand behind the guardrail.
  • Don't stand between your car and traffic.
  • At night, leave your parking lights on so Darwin can spot you.
