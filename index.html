Our Story

Two traditions, one table

Quiubo Parce brings together the best of Colombian home cooking and a Colombian panadería under one roof. From steaming bandejas built for sharing to golden bread pulled fresh from the oven, every dish is made to remind you of a table back home — or introduce you to one for the first time.

Whether you're stopping in for a full lunch, a quick empanada, or a box of pan de bono for the road, our kitchen and bakery counter are ready to say ¡Quiubo, parce! — a warm, easygoing Colombian greeting between friends.
