Our Story

Two traditions, one table

Quiubo Parce brings together the best of Colombian home cooking and a Colombian panadería under one roof. From steaming bandejas built for sharing to golden bread pulled fresh from the oven, every dish is made to remind you of a table back home — or introduce you to one for the first time.

Whether you're stopping in for a full lunch, a quick empanada, or a box of pan de bono for the road, our kitchen and bakery counter are ready to say ¡Quiubo, parce! — a warm, easygoing Colombian greeting between friends.

Menu Highlights

Plates worth the trip

A few of the dishes guests can't stop talking about.

Bandeja Paisa

The tower of the house

A generous mountain plate piled with crispy fried pork belly, red beans, grilled steak, Colombian chorizo, a fried egg, a fresh arepa, and sweet fried plantains. One look and you'll understand why it's the star of the show.

Mojarra Frita

Whole fried fish

A generously sized whole fried fish, crispy outside and tender inside, served the traditional Colombian way — simple, fresh, and full of flavor.

Arepas

Colombian staple

Warm, griddled corn cakes served alongside your meal or filled with cheese — simple comfort food done right.

Empanadas

Fried & golden

Crispy corn-dough empanadas stuffed with seasoned beef or chicken, served with fresh salsa on the side.
